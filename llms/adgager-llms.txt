# Adgager

> Adgager is a market research platform founded in 2016 with support from Istanbul Technical University (ITU). It serves brands through a verified member community and an AI-enabled technology stack. Headquarters: Koşuyolu, Kadıköy / Istanbul, Türkiye.

Adgager unifies end-to-end market research methods — from neuroscience to ethnography — on a single platform. Core building blocks are the ADTEAMS mobile community research product, a verified member community, ready-made industry reports, and a client dashboard (dash.adgager.com).
