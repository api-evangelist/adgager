## 4. How is the AdQ score calculated?

The page asks this as a heading and answers it in three sections:

### The Right Questions
"A custom question set is created specifically for advertisements. These questions help us analyze campaign effectiveness. Gager evaluations on criteria such as brand-celebrity fit, liking, and brand loyalty form the basis of this set."

### The Right Measurement
"Evaluations scored by Gagers from 1 to 10 are critical for measuring ad campaign success. These scores are combined to reflect each ad’s performance uniquely and averaged to produce a distinctive AdQ score."

### The Right Result
"This special AdQ score, used to measure the effectiveness of your ad campaigns, is generated from answers to the selected questions. It reflects each campaign’s performance in a more detailed and distinctive way. The AdQ score also lets you compare your campaigns with competitors. Comparison results help you better understand your position and strength in the market, optimize your strategies, and stay one step ahead. The AdQ score helps you understand, evaluate, and compare the impact of your campaigns more deeply. Once you understand your campaign strengths and weaknesses, you gain the opportunity to optimize your strategies more effectively."

## 7. Subscription Plan — features

- **ALL IN ONE**
- **BRAND ADQ REPORT** — "Choose this plan to monitor and analyze advertising measurement for a general brand. You can access comprehensive reports and data to identify campaign successes and improvement opportunities."
- **SUB-BRAND ADQ REPORT** — "Choose this plan to examine the advertising performance of a specific sub-brand under your main brand in detail. For example, you can use it to analyze ads for an ice cream brand under a main brand."
- **SUBCATEGORY AVG.** — "Choose this plan to evaluate the average performance of ads within a specific subcategory. For example, you can use it to analyze ads for chocolate products within fast-moving consumer goods."
- **MAIN CATEGORY AVG.** — "Choose this plan to track the overall performance of ad campaigns at main category level. You can observe the average scoring for ads in a main category such as FMCG."
- **TURKEY AVG.** — "Use this plan to review general ADQ measurement averages for all ads aired in Turkey within specific date ranges. It is useful for understanding advertising trends in Turkey and measuring competition."

- **COMPETITOR AD FILM REPORT** — "Choose this plan to examine competitor brands' ad campaigns in detail and improve your own strategy. For example, you can analyze ads from another platform competing with a food delivery platform."
